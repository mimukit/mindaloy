Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high fidelity flat UI concept board for Mindaloy, a spaced repetition web app. This is the first dark-mode design exploration requested by the owner. Use the restraint and compact sidebar organization of Mochi's dark app, with an original Mindaloy layout. No Mochi branding.
Composition: landscape board, two large equally sized desktop screenshots stacked on the left taking about 70% width, one tall phone review screenshot on the right. Plain dark neutral background, generous separation between screenshots, no perspective, no physical devices, no decorative marketing graphics. Tiny board label "Mindaloy / Dark concept 01". Show the full screen boundaries. Prioritize legible text and precise alignment.
Palette: app background #222222, sidebar #1D1D1D, panels #292929, borders #383838, main text #ECECE8, secondary text #ADADA7, muted blue action #A5BED7 with dark button text. This palette is proposed, not an existing brand. Sans serif UI typography, small restrained outline icons, 6px corner radius, thin separators, no gradients or glows.
